# Gym Diary – Design Ideas

## Approach A – Industrial Dark Performance
**Design Movement:** Industrial Brutalism meets Sports Performance
**Core Principles:** Raw power aesthetics, high contrast, data-first, no decoration for decoration's sake
**Color Philosophy:** Near-black background (#0A0A0A), electric orange (#FF5500) as the primary accent for PRs and progress, cold white for data, muted steel gray for secondary elements. Orange = fire, effort, achievement.
**Layout Paradigm:** Full-bleed mobile layout, bottom tab navigation, card-based content with hard edges and subtle inner glows
**Signature Elements:** Thin horizontal rules as separators, monospaced numbers for weights/reps, pulsing dot indicators for active day
**Interaction Philosophy:** Haptic-feel press states, quick swipe gestures, instant feedback toasts
**Animation:** Subtle scale-on-press, smooth tab transitions, progress bars animate on mount
**Typography:** Space Grotesk (headings) + JetBrains Mono (numbers/data) – industrial precision
**Probability:** 0.08

## Approach B – Obsidian Notebook
**Design Movement:** Premium Dark Notebook / Moleskine Digital
**Core Principles:** Tactile feel, warm dark tones, handcrafted precision, data as art
**Color Philosophy:** Deep obsidian background (oklch(0.10 0.008 260)), warm amber/gold (#F5A623) for highlights, soft cream for text. Feels like writing in a premium notebook under a lamp.
**Layout Paradigm:** Single-column scrollable feed with generous padding, floating bottom nav pill
**Signature Elements:** Subtle paper texture overlay, thin gold borders on key cards, weight numbers in large display type
**Interaction Philosophy:** Deliberate, calm interactions – this is a journal, not a dashboard
**Animation:** Gentle fade-ins, smooth accordion expansions, no jarring transitions
**Typography:** Playfair Display (headings) + IBM Plex Mono (data) – premium editorial
**Probability:** 0.07

## Approach C – Neon Athletic Dashboard ← CHOSEN
**Design Movement:** Dark Athletic / Modern Fitness App (Nike Training Club meets Whoop)
**Core Principles:** Performance-focused dark UI, bold typography hierarchy, color-coded data categories, mobile-first precision
**Color Philosophy:** Deep charcoal background (oklch(0.11 0.006 260)), vivid orange-red (#E84D1C) as primary action color, electric green for success/completed, cool blue for informational, muted warm gray for secondary. Each exercise category gets its own accent color.
**Layout Paradigm:** Bottom tab navigation (4 tabs), hero card at top of overview, horizontal day-selector strips, expandable exercise cards
**Signature Elements:** Large bold weight numbers (e.g. "160 kg" in 32px), category color dots, progress rings, week-view day pills
**Interaction Philosophy:** Snappy, responsive – every tap gives immediate visual feedback. Edit inline, no modal overload.
**Animation:** Smooth slide-up for expanded cards, number counter animations for stats, tab transitions with slight translate
**Typography:** Barlow Condensed (display/numbers) + Inter (body/labels) – athletic precision

---

## CHOSEN: Approach C – Neon Athletic Dashboard

Dark athletic app with bold typography, color-coded exercise categories, bottom tab navigation, and a clean mobile-first layout. The app should feel like a premium fitness tracking tool – dark background, vivid accent colors, large weight numbers, and smooth interactions.
